## Roll20: Free / Plus / Pro / Elite tiers, prices, storage, gated features, recent changes

### Takeaway

Roll20 has four tiers: Free; Plus at $5.99/mo or $59.99/yr; Pro at $10.99/mo or $109.99/yr; and Elite, added in October 2025, at $14.99/mo or $149.99/yr. Storage is 100 MB, 4 GB, 10 GB and 50 GB. Only the game creator pays: the creator's tier unlocks the features for every player in the game. The last price rise was in 2021. No regional or BRL pricing was found.

### Cited Findings

- From the official "Feature Breakdown" help article, dated November 17, 2025 — [Roll20 Help: Feature Breakdown](https://help.roll20.net/hc/articles/360037774633):
  - **Free:** "Dedicated Online Hosting with unlimited players and games"; 1,200+ game systems; owned content sharing in 1 game, up to 5 players; 100 MB upload storage; 20 MB maximum upload; 2 active LFG listings; 3 character transfers per game.
  - **Plus ($5.99/mo or $59.99/yr):** sharing in 3 games, up to 10 players each; 4 GB storage; 50 MB maximum upload (audio stays at 20 MB); unlimited LFG listings; **Dynamic Lighting**; shared table features; external journal access; unlimited character transfers; **no loadscreen ads**; **Demiplane Unlimited**; Plus badge.
  - **Pro ($10.99/mo or $109.99/yr):** sharing in 5 games, 15 players each; 10 GB storage; 100 MB maximum upload; custom game backgrounds; Roll20 Reserve rewards; free Pro token sets; **Mod (API) access**; **Transmogrifier** (moves assets between games); **custom character sheets**; early access.
  - **Elite ($14.99/mo or $149.99/yr):** sharing in 20 games, 15 players each; 50 GB storage; 100 MB maximum upload; Dungeon Scrawl Pro access; Elite badge.
- Players do not need their own subscription. "Feature access for the whole game is based on the Game Creator's current subscription." No free trials are offered for Plus or Pro. Page dated Dec 13, 2024. — [Roll20 Help: Roll20 Subscriptions](https://help.roll20.net/hc/en-us/articles/28498571071255-Roll20-Subscriptions)
- Elite was announced Oct 22, 2025 at $150/yr. It targets "high-usage" GMs, with 50 GB of storage, rulebooks shared across 20 games × 15 players (300 slots), and Dungeon Scrawl Pro (valued at $60/yr). It does not change Free, Plus or Pro. — [GeekNative, 2025-10-22](https://www.geeknative.com/210141/roll20-adds-new-150-subscription-aimed-at-high-usage-game-masters)
- 2021 price rise: Plus went from $4.99 to $5.99/mo and Pro from $99.99 to $109.99/yr. Subscribers from before July 1, 2021 kept the old rate for 2 years. — [Roll20 blog: Price Increase](https://bloghub.roll20.net/posts/roll20-pricing-increase-announcement); [GeekNative](https://www.geeknative.com/130974/roll20-to-increase-prices-offering-a-2-year-lock-in-before-they-do)
- Promotion: the 2025 "Scrawliday" sale gave 25% off an annual Pro subscription; Elite was not eligible. — [Roll20 blog: Scrawliday](https://blog.roll20.net/posts/scrawliday-dungeon-scrawl-sale/)
- Older wiki pages list Plus at 3 GB and Pro at 6 GB. These are outdated next to the Nov 2025 help article. — [Roll20 Wiki: Plus](https://wiki.roll20.net/Plus); [Roll20 Wiki: Pro](https://wiki.roll20.net/Pro)

### Inferences

- The annual plan is about 2 months free (~16.5% off) on every Roll20 tier.
- Roll20 gates on storage and the number of games/players with shared content, plus "power" features: Dynamic Lighting at Plus; Mod/API scripts, custom sheets and the Transmogrifier at Pro. The base VTT (unlimited games and players) stays free, and ads appear only on the free tier's loadscreens.
- Bundling Demiplane Unlimited into Plus suggests Roll20 now owns or integrates Demiplane (see the Demiplane section).

### Gaps

- No BRL or regional pricing found for Roll20. Its pricing page (roll20.net/pricing) is rendered client-side and could not be read directly. Checkout currency for Brazil was not verified.
- No current active-user or paid-conversion numbers (see the user-numbers section).

## Foundry VTT and hosting (The Forge, Molten)

### Takeaway

Foundry is a $50 perpetual license, bought once by the GM only, and includes all future updates. For a Brazilian visitor the official purchase page shows an estimated **R$290.49** in local currency, via Stripe. Hosting is self-hosted by default. The Forge charges $4.49–$12.99/mo for hosting on top of the Foundry license.

### Cited Findings

- "$50.00 USD" for a "Software License Key", described as "a perpetual license to use the software and have access to all future updates." For Brazil the page shows "Your total is estimated in your local currency (BRL)" = **R$290.49**, with the exact amount confirmed by Stripe at checkout. — [foundryvtt.com/purchase](https://foundryvtt.com/purchase/) (read 2026-11-10)
- Players do not need a license: "$50 once, paid only by the GM" (user comment, Mar 2026). — [r/rpg snapshot 2026-03-24](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1s233l1/snapshots/2026-03-24T17%3A53%3A21.26145Z). Individual licenses at $50 are also confirmed in the Foundry FAQ. — [Foundry FAQ](https://foundryvtt.com/article/faq/)
- **The Forge** tiers, read from the forge-vtt.com homepage on 2026-11-10 — [forge-vtt.com](https://forge-vtt.com/):
  - **Player:** free; "No Foundry license required" to join a Forge campaign.
  - **Game Master:** $4.49 monthly or $3.99/mo billed annually; unlimited worlds; import/export; 14-day free trial.
  - **Story Teller ("Most Popular"):** $8.99 monthly or $8.08/mo billed annually; adds Game Manager, advanced user management, D&D Beyond integration, and multiple games at once.
  - **World Builder:** $12.99 monthly or $11.66/mo billed annually; adds LiveKit A/V servers, Save Points, and automatic snapshots.
- Annual totals and storage, from the Forge plans page as relayed by search on 2026-11-10 — [forge-vtt.com/plans](https://forge-vtt.com/plans):
  - Game Master: $47.99/yr; 500 MB game data + 5 GB assets.
  - Story Teller: $96.99/yr; 1 GB data + 10 GB assets.
  - World Builder: $139.99/yr; 2 GB data + 20 GB assets.
  - Each tier needs a Foundry license. Extra storage costs $12.99/yr per 500 MB of data or per 5 GB of assets.
  - (The page is client-rendered; these figures came through the search tool and could not be confirmed by a direct fetch.)
- Molten Hosting is another managed Foundry host. — [moltenhosting.com](https://moltenhosting.com/) (prices not collected)
- Foundry sales: from June 2021 to May 2022 it sold "almost exactly the same number of software licenses" as in the 12 months before. No absolute count is given. — [Foundry Year in Review 2022](https://foundryvtt.com/article/year-in-review-2022/)

### Inferences

- The BRL estimate implies about R$5.81 per US$1 at checkout, with no regional discount: Brazilians pay the full USD price plus FX.
- The Forge's annual discount (~10%) is smaller than the industry's usual ~16–17%.

### Gaps

- Molten Hosting prices were not collected.
- No total of Foundry licenses sold was found. Foundry's 2025 and 2026 anniversary posts cover sales and promotions, not totals.

## Fantasy Grounds (Unity / "Fantasy Grounds VTT")

### Takeaway

SmiteWorks made Fantasy Grounds **free to play in November 2025**. The license and subscription model was retired, and any GM can host unlimited players. Money now comes from licensed rulesets/modules and cosmetics like 3D dice and art. On Steam the base app is listed as free in both BR and US.

### Cited Findings

- On Nov 10, 2025: "all users gain access to the platform without having to spend anything." Before that, hosting needed "at least $50 for an Ultimate License (or a monthly fee)." Licensed packs (Alien, D&D, Pathfinder) are still paid. — [GamingOnLinux, 2025-11-10](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/11/fantasy-grounds-virtual-tabletop-vtt-is-now-free-to-play/)
- "Previously cost a flat fee of $50… now free to play. Users get all the tools except licensed module access and cosmetics like digital dice." — [StartPlaying news, 2025-11-15](https://startplaying.games/blog/posts/fantasy-grounds-free-to-play-ghost-in-the-shell-ttrpg-netflix-dnd-show-news)
- Earlier official pricing, now historical: Standard (GM) $39 or $3.99/mo; Ultimate $149 or $9.99/mo; a free or demo tier could join only one license-holder's game. The pre-launch announcement gave the same $39 / $149. — [EN World (launch)](https://www.enworld.org/forum/showthread.php?p=7599318); [Fantasy Grounds press kit](https://www.fantasygrounds.com/press/)
- **Conflict:** news coverage says the Ultimate license was "formerly $50" ([GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/11/fantasy-grounds-virtual-tabletop-vtt-is-now-free-to-play/)), while the old official comparison listed $149. The $50 figure likely reflects a later price cut, since the forum has a "New Low Prices on Licenses" thread ([FG forums](https://www.fantasygrounds.com/forums/archive/index.php/t-81414.html)), but that could not be confirmed (403).
- After Feb 4, 2025, every FG license bought on Steam counted as Ultimate. — search summary of the [Steam Ultimate License Upgrade page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1396780/)
- Steam API, 2026-11-10: "Fantasy Grounds VTT" (app 1196310) shows `is_free: true` with cc=br. The "Ultimate License Upgrade" (app 1396780) has no price in BR or US, and its description still says the license "allows hosting games for free/Demo players." — [Steam appdetails 1196310](https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=1196310&cc=br); [Steam appdetails 1396780](https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=1396780&cc=br)
- Publishers such as Modiphius and Goodman Games backed the free pivot with content drops. — [GeekNative](https://www.geeknative.com/215245/modiphius-and-goodman-games-back-fantasy-grounds-free-to-play-pivot-with-massive-content-drops)

### Inferences

- An old one-time, paid-per-host VTT moved to free plus a content marketplace. This is evidence of strong price pressure in the VTT market.

### Gaps

- No BRL price for FG content packs on Steam was collected.
- The final pre-free Ultimate price (the $50 vs $149 conflict) is unresolved.

## Owlbear Rodeo

### Takeaway

There are three tiers: **Nestling** (free, 100 MB), **Fledgling** ($3.99/mo or $39.99/yr, 5 GB) and **Bestling** ($7.99/mo or $79.99/yr, 10 GB, more custom rooms). The prices come from the 2.0 dev log (2023), and the live pricing page could not be read. There are no ads, and paid users subsidize free users.

### Cited Findings

- New sign-ups get the free Nestling plan. Fledgling and Bestling "give you more storage and allow you to create more Rooms" and let you customize room names and backgrounds. Annual billing is available. — [Owlbear docs: Managing your Subscription](https://docs.owlbear.rodeo/docs/managing-your-subscription/)
- Dev log 6 (2023):
  - Nestling moves from $3/mo to free, with 100 MB of cloud storage.
  - Fledgling moves from $5 to "$3.99 a month or… two months free by purchasing a year subscription for $39.99", with 5 GB and all features.
  - Bestling moves from $10 to $7.99/mo or $79.99/yr, with 10 GB and more custom rooms.
  - — [Owlbear Rodeo 2.0 Dev Log 6](https://blog.owlbear.rodeo/owlbear-rodeo-2-0-dev-log-6/)
- The 2022 subscriptions post: paid subscribers cover free users' costs; the free tier has every feature needed to play; no advertising. The Oct 2026 posts mention that "Bestling and Fledgling subscribers" get automatic fog detection, and the May 2026 v2.4 release added a "storage saver". — [State of the Rodeo: Subscriptions](https://blog.owlbear.rodeo/state-of-the-rodeo-lets-talk-about-subscriptions)
- Extensions (the plugin ecosystem) are part of the platform. MCDM recommends Owlbear as a free option for Draw Steel online play. — [r/rpg snapshot 2026-03-24](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1s233l1/snapshots/2026-03-24T17%3A53%3A21.26145Z)

### Inferences

- Owlbear gates on storage and room count, not features. Since Oct 2026, a few convenience features such as auto fog detection have started to move to paid tiers.

### Gaps

- Live 2026 prices and room limits are not confirmed: owlbear.rodeo is a JS-only page and returned 403 to the fetch tool.
- Whether extensions are limited on the free tier is not confirmed.

## D&D Beyond Maps / Sigil / Above VTT

### Takeaway

D&D Beyond has three tiers: Free, Hero (from $2.17/mo annual, $2.99 monthly) and Master (from $4.58/mo annual, $5.99 monthly). Maps VTT is usable on Free ("Run games on the Maps VTT"). Master adds custom map uploads, homebrew tokens, 10 GB of storage, and content sharing to 12 people across 5 campaigns. Sigil (3D) launched free in Mar 2025, with multiplayer hosting tied to Master. Above VTT is a free extension.

### Cited Findings

- Free $0 ("Run games on the Maps VTT"). Hero "from $2.17 USD/mo" (table also shows $2.99), with 250+ VTT components from Drops. Master "from $4.58 USD/mo" (table also shows $5.99): upload custom maps, homebrew monsters on Maps, "10gb of storage", and purchased books shared with up to 12 people across 5 campaigns. USD only; prices "exclusive of applicable taxes." — [D&D Beyond: Choose Your Plan](https://www.dndbeyond.com/en/subscribe) (read 2026-11-10)
- Master Tier lets you upload custom and animated maps and use your own homebrew monster tokens. — [D&D Beyond support: Master Tier](https://dndbeyond-support.wizards.com/hc/en-us/articles/46363179646356-What-does-a-Master-Tier-subscription-give-me)
- A May 2026 report: Hero and Master prices stay the same while weekly "Drops" content is added. — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/tabletop-gaming/d-and-d-launches-game-pass-style-subscription-system-with-free-encounters-maps-and-more/); [EN World](https://www.enworld.org/threads/drops-will-make-d-d-beyond-subscribers-love-thursdays.719035/page-12)
- Sigil launched Mar 1, 2025 as free to any D&D Beyond account. Hosting multiplayer sessions and painting minis need Master Tier. — [OpenCritic news, 2025-03-01](https://opencritic.com/news/13241/sigil-d-ds-long-awaited-3d-vtt-is-finally-available-with-a-very-surprising-price-point)
- Above VTT is free; a 2026 roundup calls it "more of a Chrome extension," working with free D&D Beyond accounts but only for D&D. A user claims "no patreon-only features" (informal). — [StartPlaying: list of VTTs](https://startplaying.games/blog/posts/a-comprehensive-list-of-virtual-tabletops); [D&D Beyond forums](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/general-discussion/70327-how-about-finishing-the-vtt?comment=62)

### Inferences

- $4.58 × 12 ≈ $54.99/yr for Master and $2.17 × 12 ≈ $25.99/yr for Hero. These are inferred totals; the page shows only per-month figures. The annual discount is about 23–27%.

### Gaps

- Sigil's status after March 2025 was not verified.
- Above VTT has no official pricing page.

- The "every player pays" model (TTS, TaleSpire, Draw Steel Codex) draws visible user resistance. The GM-pays model (Roll20, Foundry, Forge, Owlbear) is the norm.

### Gaps

- Shard's and Alchemy's current official prices are not confirmed: their pricing pages returned 404.
- Let's Role Traveler/Adventurer prices are missing.
- Current Demiplane standalone price and date are missing.

## What free tiers typically limit; annual discounts; semiannual plans

### Takeaway

Free tiers almost never gate the core VTT: maps, tokens, dice, unlimited players and games are free on Roll20, Owlbear, FG, Alchemy and Shard. They gate **storage** (100–125 MB free vs 4–50 GB paid), **upload size**, **number of shared-content games/players**, **advanced automation** (Roll20 Dynamic Lighting at Plus; Mod/API, custom sheets and the Transmogrifier at Pro), **rooms or campaign count** (Owlbear, old Alchemy), and **ads/cosmetics**. The annual discount is usually about 16–17% ("two months free"). No VTT found offers a semiannual plan.

### Cited Findings

- Free storage: Roll20 100 MB with a 20 MB max upload ([Roll20](https://help.roll20.net/hc/articles/360037774633)); Owlbear 100 MB ([Dev Log 6](https://blog.owlbear.rodeo/owlbear-rodeo-2-0-dev-log-6/)); Let's Role 125 MB ([Let's Role](https://lets-role.com/blog/subscription-changes-119)).
- Paid storage: Roll20 4 / 10 / 50 GB; Owlbear 5 / 10 GB; Forge 5 / 10 / 20 GB of assets; D&D Beyond Master 10 GB. — sources above.
- Content-sharing limits: Roll20 Free 1 game × 5 players, Plus 3 × 10, Pro 5 × 15, Elite 20 × 15 ([Roll20](https://help.roll20.net/hc/articles/360037774633)); D&D Beyond Master 12 people / 5 campaigns ([DDB](https://www.dndbeyond.com/en/subscribe)).
- Ads: Roll20 free shows loadscreen ads, removed in Plus ([Roll20](https://help.roll20.net/hc/articles/360037774633)); Owlbear has no ads ([Owlbear blog](https://blog.owlbear.rodeo/state-of-the-rodeo-lets-talk-about-subscriptions)); Let's Role removes mobile ads with Play Pass ([Let's Role](https://lets-role.com/blog/subscription-changes-119)).
- Annual discount, computed from the cited prices:
  - Roll20: 16.5–16.6% on all tiers.
  - Owlbear: about 16.5% ("two months free").
  - Forge: about 10–11%.
  - D&D Beyond: about 23–27%.
  - Quest Portal Pro: about 25%.
  - Alchemy: about 8% (unconfirmed price).
  - Let's Role Explorer: annual only.

### Inferences

- The "two months free" (~16.7%) annual discount is the clear market convention. Semiannual billing does not appear in any VTT pricing found.
- The low end of paid tiers clusters at $3.99–$5.99/mo (Owlbear, Forge GM, Roll20 Plus, D&D Beyond Master monthly). The "pro" GM tier sits at $8–$11/mo, and the 2025 high-end tier (Roll20 Elite) at about $15/mo.

### Gaps

- No semiannual plan was found. This is an absence of evidence, not a confirmed survey of every checkout page.

## User numbers and conversion to paid

### Takeaway

Public figures are old and are registered accounts, not active users: Roll20 claimed 10 million users in early 2022. No source gives a paid-conversion rate, a subscriber count, or a total of Foundry licenses sold.

### Cited Findings

- Roll20 passed 10 million users (early 2022, a Kickstarter-anniversary press release). Forum commenters note this counts accounts ever created. — [EN World: Roll20 Now Has 10 Million Users](https://www.enworld.org/threads/roll20-now-has-10-million-users.686259/)
- Earlier milestones: 5 million users ([Roll20 blog](https://bloghub.roll20.net/posts/5-million-users-roll20-finds-critical-success/)) and 2 million ([EN World](https://www.enworld.org/threads/roll20-hits-2-million-users.664069/page-2)).
- D&D makes up more than 60% of Roll20 games (from the Orr Group industry report). — [Bell of Lost Souls](https://www.belloflostsouls.net/?p=346067). A secondary blog says the Orr Group Industry Report has been on hold since 2022 ([voxbooster, 2026](https://voxbooster.com/blog/dnd-ttrpg-statistics-2026)), not independently verified.
- Foundry: license sales from June 2021 to May 2022 were flat year over year, with no absolute count. — [Foundry Year in Review 2022](https://foundryvtt.com/article/year-in-review-2022/)
- Let's Role (2023): visits and revenue were rising but short of the company's goals; no figures given. — [EN World](https://www.enworld.org/threads/lets-role-virtual-tabletop-ceases-new-development-not-to-be-confused-with-role-virtual-tabletop.700998)

### Inferences

- Without published conversion data, any freemium-to-paid assumption for VTTs needs analogies from outside this research.

### Gaps

- No 2024–2026 user counts for Roll20, Foundry, Owlbear or D&D Beyond.
- No paid-conversion rates for any VTT.
- SimilarWeb/Semrush traffic for roll20.net was not collected ([Semrush overview](https://semrush.com/website/roll20.net/overview) exists, Dec 2025).
