# Contracts · backoffice e links de conta

Detalhe do hub [`../contracts.md`](../contracts.md): os DTOs de `src/admin/` e `src/account-links/`. As rotas estão em [`../user-admin.md`](../user-admin.md), [`../account-links.md`](../account-links.md) e [`../sanctions.md`](../sanctions.md).

- `admin-users.dto.ts` — `adminUserDto` (`{ id, username, name, email, role, plan (o do admin), paidPlan e paidPlanUntil (o pago em vigor, só leitura), lorkariumOwner, status: active | suspended, color, avatarId, campaignCount, lastSeenAt | null, createdAt }`), `adminInvitationDto` (`{ id, email, role, invitedBy (@ | null), sentAt, expiresAt }`; "vencido" é do client), `adminDirectoryResponseDto` `{ users, invitations }`, `adminUserResponseDto` `{ user }`, `adminUserParamsDto` `{ userId: uuid }`, `updateUserRoleRequestDto` `{ role }` e `updateUserPlanRequestDto` `{ plan }` estritos, `blockingCampaignDto` `{ id, name, otherMembers ≥ 1 }`, `userDeletionCheckDto` `{ blockingCampaigns, soloCampaigns }`.
- `admin-invitations.dto.ts` — `createInvitationRequestDto` `{ email (trim + minúsculas), role }` estrito, `invitationParamsDto`, `invitationLinkResponseDto` `{ invitation, link }`, `passwordResetLinkResponseDto` `{ link, expiresAt }`.
- `account-links/account-links.dto.ts` — `accountLinkTokenDto` `{ token }` (sobre `linkTokenDto` de `account/account-recovery.dto.ts`), `invitationOriginDto` (`admin` | `self`: o backoffice ou o cadastro público), `invitationInspectResponseDto` `{ email, suggestedUsername, origin }`, `acceptInvitationRequestDto` `{ token, username (usernameDto), password (newPasswordDto), persistent, acceptedTerms: true }` (literal: a caixa dos Termos e da Privacidade desmarcada é 400), `acceptInvitationResponseDto` `{ status: 'signed-in', user }`, `passwordResetInspectResponseDto` `{ username }`, `completePasswordResetRequestDto` `{ token, newPassword }`.
- `account-links/sign-up.dto.ts` — `humanCheckTokenDto` (o token do Cloudflare Turnstile, 1 a 2048), `signUpRequestDto` `{ email (aparado, minúsculo), humanCheckToken }` (`POST /api/sign-up`; estrito: só o endereço, o @ e a senha vêm com o link), `passwordResetRequestDto` `{ identifier (loginIdentifierDto: e-mail ou @), humanCheckToken }` (`POST /api/password-resets/request`) e `signUpSettingsDto` `{ registrationOpen, humanCheckSiteKey (nulo sem chave fora da produção) }` (`GET /api/sign-up/settings`).
- `admin-mail-samples.dto.ts` — `mailSampleDto` `{ id, name, subject }`, `mailSamplesResponseDto` `{ samples }`, `mailSampleParamsDto` `{ sampleId }`, `sendMailSampleRequestDto` `{ to }` (aparado, minúsculas), `sendMailSampleResponseDto` `{ subject, to }`.
- `account-sanction.dto.ts` — medidas contra contas (`SANCTION_KINDS`, `SANCTION_RULES`/`SANCTION_RULE_SECTIONS`, pedido, contestação, histórico): [`../sanctions.md`](../sanctions.md).
- Specs: `packages/contracts/test/admin/*.spec.ts`, `test/account-links/{account-links,sign-up}.dto.spec.ts`.
